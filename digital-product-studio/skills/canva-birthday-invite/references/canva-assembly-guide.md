# Canva Assembly Guide

Use this handoff after the invitation visuals are complete.

## Prepare the Canva design

1. Open Canva and create a Website design or a custom-size design using the customer's exact width, height, and unit. Canva custom dimensions may be entered in pixels, inches, millimeters, or centimeters when supported by the selected design flow.
2. Upload every generated invitation section and transparent asset.
3. Place the closed-envelope image first and the matching opened-envelope image second. Set both to the exact same page size and align them to identical x/y coordinates, scale, and crop.
4. Add a brief Canva page transition or animation between the two images so the closed envelope appears to open. Prefer a restrained dissolve, match-and-move style transition when available, or a very short fade. Preview it and remove any motion that makes the envelope jump, resize, or drift.
5. Arrange the hero cover and remaining sections after the two envelope images. Confirm every section uses the approved orientation and aspect ratio. If the generated artwork used a closest matching aspect ratio, place it inside the exact Canva canvas without stretching; scale proportionally and crop only expendable outer background areas.
6. Use Canva text boxes for final names, dates, addresses, schedules, and detailed instructions whenever possible.

## Add clickable interactions

Select a button or grouped button graphic, choose the link option in Canva, and add the user's real destination.

Recommended links:

- RSVP → Google Forms, Tally, Jotform, Typeform, email, text, or another supplied form
- Directions → Google Maps or Apple Maps
- Playlist → Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, or another supplied playlist
- Gift button → registry, wishlist, payment page, or gift-information page
- Calendar → a user-created calendar-event link
- Gallery → another Canva page or external gallery

Do not invent links.

## Countdown section

The generated countdown section is a visual design. Replace the placeholder numbers with:

- Static numbers updated manually,
- A countdown video or GIF imported into Canva, or
- A Canva-supported countdown element available in the user's account.

Do not describe a static countdown as live.

## Gallery

Place the celebrant's real photos into the prepared Canva frames. Check that faces and important details are not cropped.

## Music

Use a clickable play button linked to a playlist, or add supported audio/video media in Canva. Confirm that music behavior works in the published format before selling or sharing the invitation.

## Mobile check

Before publishing:

- Confirm the closed envelope appears first and the opened envelope appears second.
- Scrub between the first two pages and verify the envelope does not jump, shift, resize, rotate, recolor, or change decorations.
- Confirm the transition clearly reads as one envelope opening to reveal the invitation.
- Preview on a phone.
- Confirm the text is readable.
- Confirm no content is cut off.
- Confirm the published or exported design matches the customer's requested dimensions.
- Confirm artwork was not stretched or distorted during resizing.
- Test every link.
- Check the scroll order.
- Confirm all customer placeholders were replaced.

## Publish

Use Canva's Website publishing option. Choose the publishing style that best matches the design and test the public link before sharing it.
