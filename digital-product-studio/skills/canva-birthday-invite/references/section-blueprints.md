# Section Blueprints

Use these as flexible defaults. Keep copy short and leave room for editable Canva text.

## 1. Closed envelope — mandatory

Purpose: Create the invitation's first visible moment and set up the opening reveal.

Include:
- One complete closed envelope as the main subject
- Theme-matched envelope color and material
- Optional wax seal, bow, ribbon, monogram, florals, pearls, or small themed accent
- A clean background and enough open space for Canva animation

Lock the envelope's position, scale, perspective, lighting, shadows, decorations, crop, and background for the next image.

Avoid:
- Showing the envelope already open
- Showing the full invitation card
- Combining closed and open states in one image
- Replacing the envelope with a small icon

## 2. Opened envelope reveal — mandatory

Purpose: Create the second visible moment and make the envelope appear to open.

Use the finished closed-envelope image as the image-editing reference. Keep the same canvas and scene. Change only:
- Open the flap
- Reveal or partially raise the invitation card
- Add a short approved heading, name, monogram, or age on the card when desired

Do not change the envelope's position, scale, perspective, materials, lighting, shadows, decorations, crop, or background. This image must align directly over the closed version in Canva.

## 3. Hero cover

Purpose: Immediately communicate who, what, and when.

Include:
- Birthday headline
- Celebrant name
- Age or milestone
- Date and time
- Primary themed artwork
- Optional “Scroll for details” cue

Avoid:
- Full address
- Long paragraphs
- Too many decorations

## 4. Welcome message

Purpose: Set the mood and invite guests warmly.

Include:
- Short welcome headline
- Two- or three-sentence invitation message
- Optional portrait or decorative frame

## 5. Event details

Purpose: Present essential information clearly.

Include visual spaces for:
- Date
- Time
- Venue
- Address
- Host name

Use icons sparingly.

## 6. Countdown

Purpose: Create anticipation.

Include:
- “Counting down to…” headline
- Four styled number boxes
- Labels: Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds
- Optional note that the final numbers or media can be added in Canva

Do not claim the generated image is a live countdown.

## 7. Dress code and guest notes

Purpose: Clarify expectations.

Include:
- Dress-code title
- Color swatches or outfit mood icons
- Two to four short guest notes

Possible notes:
- Adults only
- Arrive by a certain time
- Parking information
- No boxed gifts
- Bring identification

Use placeholders when details are unknown.

## 8. Schedule or itinerary

Purpose: Show the event flow.

Include:
- Three to six time slots
- Short event labels
- Clean vertical timeline or cards

Avoid dense paragraphs.

## 9. Venue and directions

Purpose: Help guests find the event.

Include:
- Venue name
- Address placeholder
- Decorative map-frame placeholder
- “Get Directions” button graphic

Never invent a map or exact location.

## 10. RSVP

Purpose: Direct guests to respond.

Include:
- RSVP headline
- Deadline
- One main button graphic
- Optional visual fields for name, attendance, guest count, and message

Keep real form behavior outside the generated image. The user adds the destination link in Canva.

## 11. Photo gallery

Purpose: Personalize the invitation.

Include:
- Three to six photo frames
- Short gallery headline
- Optional “Tap to view more” cue if linked later in Canva

Do not invent photos of the celebrant.

## 12. Gift registry or gift note

Purpose: Communicate gifting preferences gracefully.

Include:
- Short gift message
- Registry or payment button graphic
- Optional “Your presence is the greatest gift” wording

Do not invent account information or payment handles.

## 13. Music or playlist

Purpose: Extend the theme and mood.

Include:
- Playlist cover area
- “Play the birthday playlist” button graphic
- Small music-themed decorations

The user links the button in Canva.

## 14. Closing and hashtag

Purpose: End warmly and reinforce the event identity.

Include:
- Thank-you message
- Celebrant name or monogram
- Social hashtag placeholder
- RSVP reminder or final button
- Light closing artwork
