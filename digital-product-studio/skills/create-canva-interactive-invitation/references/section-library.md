# Adaptive Section Library

Use sections only when they fit the event or the user explicitly requests them.

## Closed envelope
A themed closed envelope that introduces the invitation. Match the event's formality and visual style.

## Opened envelope
The exact matching envelope after opening, revealing a card or paper insert. Do not add labels or instructions inside the image.

## Cover or hero
The event name, honoree, couple, organization, or central announcement. Keep it visually memorable and text-light.

## Welcome or announcement
A short emotional or informational introduction appropriate to the event.

## Event details
Date, start time, end time if supplied, venue, address, host, and essential attendance information.

## Countdown
A decorative countdown section with a clear placeholder area for Canva countdown elements. Use only for future events where anticipation is part of the experience.

## Schedule, timeline, agenda, or order of service
Present the event flow in chronological order. Use event-appropriate naming.

## Dress code
Describe attire, color palette, theme, uniform, or formality. Do not include when attire is irrelevant.

## Directions, travel, parking, and accommodations
Include only supplied information. Use map-style decorative elements without inventing directions.

## Registry, gifts, contributions, donations, or wish list
Use wording appropriate to the event. Do not pressure guests or invent registry links.

## Hosts, honorees, wedding party, speakers, clergy, sponsors, or partners
Highlight people or organizations involved. Preserve supplied names, titles, and logos.

## Menu, refreshments, or potluck
Show meal type, menu, dietary note, or what guests should bring when provided.

## Gallery, memories, or story
Use supplied photos or tasteful placeholders. Do not fabricate personal memories.

## FAQ
Use for age restrictions, plus-ones, children, accessibility, parking, weather plans, or event policies when supplied.

## Livestream or virtual access
Provide a clear placeholder for a link, QR code, access code, or platform. Do not invent credentials.

## Tickets, admission, registration, or donation
Show price or call to action only when supplied.

## RSVP
Include deadline, contact method, link placeholder, QR placeholder, or response instructions.

## Playlist
Use only when requested or when music is a central part of the invitation experience.

## Closing
End with a thank-you, anticipation message, blessing, remembrance, or contact note appropriate to the event.
