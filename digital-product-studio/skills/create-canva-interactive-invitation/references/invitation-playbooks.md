# Invitation Type Playbooks

Select the closest playbook and adapt it to the user's exact event. Do not force every section into every project.

## Wedding

Recommended sections:
- Closed envelope
- Opened envelope
- Couple or event cover
- Welcome or love-story introduction
- Ceremony and reception details
- Countdown
- Schedule or weekend itinerary
- Wedding party or hosts
- Dress code
- Venue, map, parking, travel, and accommodations
- Registry or gift note
- Gallery
- FAQ
- RSVP
- Closing

## Engagement party

Recommended sections:
- Envelope reveal
- Cover
- Announcement
- Party details
- Dress code
- Directions
- Gift note if applicable
- RSVP
- Closing

## Bridal shower or bachelorette event

Recommended sections:

## Baby shower, sprinkle, or gender reveal

Recommended sections:
- Envelope reveal
- Cover
- Welcome or baby announcement
- Event details
- Theme or dress code if relevant
- Games or schedule
- Registry or diaper-fund information
- Directions
- RSVP
- Closing

Use inclusive wording that matches the family's preferences. Do not assume gender, family structure, or parenting roles.

## Memorial, funeral, celebration of life, or remembrance

Recommended sections:
- Optional restrained envelope reveal
- Honoree cover
- Memorial announcement
- Service details
- Order of service
- Biography, tribute, or remembrance
- Photo gallery or memories
- Repast or reception details
- Directions or livestream
- Flowers, donations, or family requests
- Closing acknowledgment

Use a calm, respectful design. Avoid celebratory birthday motifs, playful typography, or excessive decoration unless the family specifically requests them.

## Religious ceremony or community event

Examples include baptism, christening, first communion, bar or bat mitzvah, church anniversary, worship night, ministry event, and faith-based conference.

Recommended sections:
- Envelope reveal
- Cover
- Welcome or scripture/theme statement
- Ceremony or event details
- Program or schedule
- Venue and directions
- Dress guidance if needed
- Hosts, clergy, speakers, or sponsors
- RSVP or registration
- Reception or fellowship details
- Closing

Use only the faith-specific language and symbols supplied or clearly requested by the user.

## Business, nonprofit, fundraiser, conference, launch, grand opening, or networking event

Recommended sections:
- Optional envelope reveal or branded opening
- Event cover
- Purpose and value statement
- Date, time, venue, and admission details
- Agenda or schedule
- Speakers, hosts, sponsors, or partners
- Dress code if relevant
- Tickets, registration, or donation information
- Directions, parking, or virtual-access details
- FAQ
- Call to action or RSVP
- Closing and contact information

Prioritize brand consistency and information clarity over decorative styling.
