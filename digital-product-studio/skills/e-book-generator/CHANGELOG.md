# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Initial release

- Supports any exact page count requested by the user.
- Supports any theme, palette, size, orientation, and writing tone.
- Supports uploaded reference e-books, PDFs, image sets, screenshots, and ZIPs.
- Adds a neutral minimalist editorial preset inspired by the supplied example.
- Requires separate full-size page generation and exact-count validation.
- Supports batching up to 10 images at a time without stopping early.
- Includes content planning, design-system locking, proofreading, PDF compilation, and ZIP packaging instructions.
- Supports optional sellable-product extras such as workbooks, listing copy, mockups, and promotional content.
