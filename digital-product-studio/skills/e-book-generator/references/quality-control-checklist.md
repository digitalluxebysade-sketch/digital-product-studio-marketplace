# Final Quality-Control Checklist

## Content

- Exact requested page count is complete.
- All requested chapters and extras are present.
- No lorem ipsum or placeholders remain.
- No repeated paragraphs or duplicate page concepts.
- Spelling, punctuation, and grammar are correct.
- Claims, data, and quotations are sourced where necessary.
- The conclusion fulfills the promise made in the introduction.

## Design

- Every page uses the exact requested dimensions.
- The visual system is consistent across all batches.
- Fonts and hierarchy feel coordinated.
- Body text is readable at normal viewing size.
- Safe margins are consistent.
- Page numbers are correct and sequential.
- Headers and footers are aligned consistently.
- Images are relevant and correctly cropped.
- No external mockup background appears on final page files.
- No accidental watermark, logo, URL, or unrelated text appears.

## Packaging

- Individual pages are zero-padded and ordered.
- The PDF order matches the individual page order.
- The PDF page count equals the manifest page count.
- The ZIP contains only approved final files.
- File names are clean and customer-friendly.
