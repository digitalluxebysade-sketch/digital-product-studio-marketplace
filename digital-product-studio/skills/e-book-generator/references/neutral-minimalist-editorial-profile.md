# Neutral Minimalist Editorial Style Profile

This profile captures the general visual system of the user's uploaded finance-guide example without copying its text, photographs, author information, or branded details.

## Visual direction

- Warm ivory, parchment, oatmeal, or soft cream background.
- Charcoal, black, or deep gray text.
- High-contrast fashion-editorial serif for major titles and chapter headings.
- Neutral sans-serif for body text, small labels, and practical information.
- Thin gray or charcoal rules to divide sections.
- Generous outer margins and calm negative space.
- Minimal ornamentation.
- Photography is warm, natural, lifestyle-focused, and placed in crisp rectangular frames.

## Cover layout

- Small letter-spaced eyebrow line near the center.
- One small portrait or lifestyle image in a thin rectangular frame above the title.
- Oversized two-line serif title as the focal point.
- Letter-spaced sans-serif subtitle.
- Thin rules around the author or brand line.
- Optional website or brand URL in small text near the bottom.
- Subtle vertical rules near the top and bottom edge may be used as a recurring motif.

## Contents page

- Very large serif “Contents” title near the upper third.
- A thin horizontal line may pass behind or through the title.
- Chapter names aligned left.
- Page numbers aligned right.
- Long chapter subtitles wrap cleanly beneath a short chapter label.
- Small editorial footer and page number at the bottom.

## About page

- Small serif page title.
- Wide framed photograph.
- One or two balanced biography columns.
- Bold sans-serif names above body copy.
- Optional small social handle beneath each biography.

## Introduction and chapter openers

- Small serif section label near the top.
- Thin horizontal rule.
- Large uppercase serif statement or chapter title.
- Body copy begins below with generous spacing.
- Chapter openers may use two columns, a side image, or a large pull quote.

## Interior article pages

- Alternate between one-column and two-column structures.
- Use bold mini-subheads to break up body copy.
- Keep lines relatively short and readable.
- Use occasional framed photography, icon rows, or simple diagrams.
- Avoid filling every page with the same composition.

## Footer system

- Small book title or section label.
- Author or brand line.
- Website or social handle if supplied by the user.
- Two-digit page number aligned to the outer bottom corner.

## Avoid

- bright neon palettes
- glossy gradients
- thick shadows
- rounded app-style cards
- busy scrapbook decoration
- oversized cartoon stickers
- more than one or two accent colors
- tiny unreadable body text
- inconsistent footer placement
