# Etsy Product Factory Automation

## Writing-First Worksheet Layout Rule

Design every worksheet and functional planner page around practical writing space. Do not make headings, decorative boxes, or individual sections unnecessarily large. Use a balanced, compact layout with about 4–7 useful sections when the page type supports it. Keep section titles small, clear, and easy to read; use thin borders, compact labels, efficient spacing, and consistent compact padding.

Use writing areas for about 60–75% of the page and decorative elements for no more than about 10–15%. Avoid oversized blank boxes that only fit one or two items. Divide large areas into useful lines, checkboxes, grids, or columns. Do not crowd the page or waste space. Keep decorations small and around the edges rather than centered over the worksheet. Do not simplify a worksheet by removing useful sections: when space is limited, reduce decorations, heading size, padding, and border thickness before reducing writing space. A side margin is allowed only on the cover page; every interior worksheet must be full-canvas with no exterior side margin.

## Purpose

Turn one user prompt into a complete, polished, sellable Etsy digital-product package.

This automation must create the actual product and its selling materials. It must not stop at brainstorming, outlines, generic recommendations, sample pages, or a single preview unless the user explicitly requests only those items.

The customer-facing product, listing, instructions, and marketing images must never mention this internal skill or call the product a skill. Describe it as a digital product, template, toolkit, automation system, printable, planner, workbook, guide, or other accurate product type.

## Core Outcome

For a full-product request, deliver an organized package containing as applicable:

1. Finished product files.
2. Every requested page as its own separate file.
3. A compiled PDF when the product is PDF-based.
4. Editable-source or access-link instructions when applicable.
5. Etsy listing images.
6. Product mockups.
7. Etsy title, description, tags, and listing details.
8. Customer thank-you and download instructions.
9. License terms selected by the user.
10. A product manifest and final quality-assurance report.
11. A final ZIP folder ready for the seller to upload or distribute.

## Supported Product Types

Use this automation for digital products such as:

- Digital planners, journals, trackers, calendars, and notebooks.
- Workbooks, worksheets, checklists, guides, e-books, and mini-courses.
- Preschool busy books, activity books, coloring books, kids' books, and learning pages.
- Canva templates, invitation sets, social-media templates, content kits, and brand kits.
- Printable wall art, cards, signs, labels, stickers, games, and party materials.
- Prompt packs, business toolkits, creator resources, seller kits, and content calendars.
- Mockup packs and product-presentation bundles.
- KDP interiors and cover concepts when requested.
- Other lawful, original digital downloads suitable for Etsy.

## One-Prompt Intake

The user may provide only a short prompt. Extract or intelligently infer:

- Product type.
- Niche and target customer.
- Main transformation or benefit.
- Theme and visual style.
- Dimensions and orientation.
- Page or asset count.
- File formats.
- Editable or non-editable status.
- Personal-use, commercial-use, or PLR rights.
- Requested listing-image count.
- Requested mockup count.
- Brand requirements.

Do not repeatedly ask questions the user has already answered. When noncritical information is missing, choose commercially sensible defaults and record those choices in the product manifest. Ask a question only when the missing information makes safe or accurate completion impossible.

## Brand and Style Handling

Honor the user's requested theme first.

When the user requests Digital Luxe by Sadé branding or gives no brand direction for a Digital Luxe product, use a polished feminine digital-boutique style. Preferred visual language may include soft pink, cream, pastel green, brown accents, gingham, polka dots, satin bows, scalloped or lace details, glossy buttons, cute Y2K touches, and a clean soft-luxury finish.

Never overcrowd the page. Prioritize clear hierarchy, legible text, functional spacing, and a premium product presentation.

If the user supplies a character or person reference:

- Preserve the approved face, skin tone, hairstyle, body features, and character identity.
- Do not alter the person's appearance unless specifically requested.
- Keep the character consistent across the project.
- Do not place the character on every listing image unless requested.

## Mandatory End-to-End Workflow

### Phase 1: Product Opportunity Brief

Create a concise internal brief covering:

- Product concept.
- Buyer problem.
- Target customer.
- Product promise.
- Distinguishing angle.
- Recommended scope.
- Deliverable formats.
- Potential add-ons or bonuses.

When current marketplace information is available through browsing tools, validate demand, terminology, seasonal timing, and competing product patterns. Do not copy competitor wording, artwork, layouts, trademarks, or proprietary products.

### Phase 2: Product Manifest

Before generating pages or assets, create a numbered manifest containing every required deliverable.

For multi-page products, list every page by number, title, purpose, dimensions, and file name. The manifest controls the exact count. A style shell, navigation shell, sample, thumbnail, preview, mockup, or blank placeholder does not count as a finished page.

Do not reduce the requested count to save usage. Continue in numbered batches until every manifest item is complete.

### Phase 3: Content Creation

Create complete, useful, customer-ready content for every item in the manifest.

Requirements:

- No placeholder text.
- No repeated filler pages presented as unique pages.
- No unfinished prompts unless prompts are the product itself.
- No generic sample content when the user requested a finished product.
- Use clear headings, useful instructions, and functional page structures.
- Proofread all visible wording.
- Keep content age-appropriate for children's products.
- Avoid medical, legal, financial, or other regulated claims that cannot be supported.

### Phase 4: Image and Page Generation

Use ChatGPT image generation for original covers, pages, interiors, illustrations, transparent assets, listing graphics, and mockups when visual generation is part of the product.

#### Separate-Page Image Generation Rule

For planners, workbooks, books, busy books, activity books, coloring books, journals, KDP interiors, invitation sections, templates, and all other multi-page projects:

- One requested page equals one separate image-generation output.
- Never combine multiple finished pages into a collage, contact sheet, grid, preview board, or one multi-page image.
- Generate all required pages.
- Use the image tool's available batch capacity efficiently, including up to ten separate images in one batch when supported.
- For projects larger than the batch limit, continue with Batch 2, Batch 3, and so on until the exact count is complete.
- Clearly identify the page number represented by each generated file.
- Do not stop after the first page or first batch.

#### Canvas Equals Product Page Rule

For full-page planner, workbook, printable, or book-page designs:

- The image canvas and the functional page must be the same object.
- The functional design must extend to the full requested canvas.
- Do not place a smaller page on top of a decorative exterior background.
- Do not show side margins, black bars, desk backgrounds, mats, frames, shadows, or rounded page corners revealing a background unless the user explicitly asks for a mockup.
- Decorative patterns may appear only as intentional design elements within the functional page.

#### Consistency Rules

- Lock dimensions, margins, typography system, palette, navigation, tabs, recurring headers, and recurring footer elements before generating the full set.
- Keep approved tabs and navigation identical on every applicable page.
- Preserve approved work.
- When one element is wrong, regenerate or edit only the affected page or region whenever possible.
- Never restart the entire set for a small targeted correction.
- Recheck text spelling, page count, composition, and dimensions after every correction.

### Phase 5: Product Assembly

Organize the finished product using this structure when applicable:

- `00_START_HERE`
- `01_PRODUCT_FILES`
- `02_SEPARATE_PAGES`
- `03_EDITABLE_LINKS_OR_SOURCE`
- `04_LISTING_IMAGES`
- `05_MOCKUPS`
- `06_LISTING_COPY`
- `07_CUSTOMER_DELIVERY`
- `08_LICENSE`
- `09_MANIFEST_AND_QA`

Use clear file names with lowercase or title-safe characters. Number multi-page files with leading zeros, such as:

- `page_001_cover.png`
- `page_002_welcome.png`
- `page_003_monthly_goals.png`

For PDF products:

- Keep every separate finished page.
- Compile pages in manifest order.
- Confirm orientation and dimensions are consistent.
- Preserve high image quality.
- Test that the PDF opens correctly.
- Add hyperlinks when requested and verify every link target.

For Canva or external-link products:

- Create a delivery PDF or text file containing the access-link placeholder or approved link.
- Include clear instructions for opening, copying, editing, saving, and exporting.
- Never invent a working share link.

### Phase 6: Etsy Listing Images

Create the exact number requested. When the user does not specify a count, create five polished listing-image concepts or finished images depending on available tools.

Recommended five-image sequence:

1. Hero image with product name and strongest benefit.
2. What's included.
3. How the product works.
4. Product mockups or inside-page preview.
5. Download, compatibility, and important purchase details.

For a ten-image set, add:

6. Buyer use cases.
7. File types, sizes, and dimensions.
8. Key features or automation steps.
9. FAQ or before-you-buy information.
10. Final lifestyle or value-focused callout.

Listing-image rules:

- Keep text brief and readable at thumbnail size.
- Do not overcrowd.
- Use accurate mockups.
- Do not claim features that are not included.
- Do not mention internal skill files or instructions.
- Show digital delivery clearly.
- Use consistent branding across the image set.
- Keep one image as the strongest thumbnail candidate.

### Phase 7: Mockups

Create realistic, polished mockups appropriate to the product, such as:

- Tablet or phone views for digital planners.
- Laptop or desktop views for templates and guides.
- Printed-page scenes for printables.
- Book or workbook views for PDFs.
- Frame or room views for wall art.
- Product thumbnails and folder views for digital bundles.

Mockups may show multiple pages because they are promotional scenes, but the original finished product pages must still be delivered separately.

Do not misrepresent a digital product as a shipped physical item.

### Phase 8: Etsy Listing Copy

Create a complete listing package containing:

- A clear keyword-rich title written for humans.
- A concise opening hook.
- A detailed product description.
- What's included.
- File formats and dimensions.
- How it works.
- Editing and compatibility information.
- Personal-use, commercial-use, or PLR terms.
- Instant-download statement.
- “No physical item will be shipped” statement.
- Important purchase notes.
- FAQ.
- Etsy search tags using the platform's current allowable quantity when known.
- Suggested category and attributes.
- Suggested price range with reasoning when requested.

Avoid keyword stuffing, false urgency, guaranteed-income claims, unsupported superlatives, or misleading claims.

### Phase 9: Customer Delivery Materials

Create:

- Thank-you message.
- Start-here instructions.
- Download and unzip instructions.
- Canva or external-access instructions when applicable.
- Basic troubleshooting.
- Contact-support wording.
- License summary.

The instructions must be simple enough for a first-time digital-product buyer.

### Phase 10: License

Use only the rights requested by the seller:

- Personal use.
- Limited commercial use.
- Commercial use.
- PLR or resell rights.

Do not silently grant PLR, resale, redistribution, or trademark rights. Clearly state what the customer may and may not do.

### Phase 11: Final Quality Assurance

Before delivery, verify:

- Exact page and asset count matches the manifest.
- Every page is separate.
- Every file is named and ordered correctly.
- Dimensions and orientation are correct.
- Product pages fill the intended canvas.
- No exterior background appears around full-page designs.
- Text is legible and proofread.
- No placeholders remain.
- No duplicate pages are counted as unique content.
- Approved character identity and brand style are preserved.
- Listing images accurately represent the product.
- Description, file types, sizes, and usage rights are accurate.
- PDF opens and pages are in order.
- Hyperlinks work when included.
- ZIP opens and folder structure is complete.
- No internal instructions, hidden prompts, or skill references appear in customer-facing files.

If a check fails, correct only the affected item and rerun the relevant checks.

## Product-Idea Mode

When the user asks for Etsy product ideas instead of a full product:

1. Generate commercially specific ideas, not vague categories.
2. For each idea, include target buyer, problem solved, deliverables, visual angle, estimated scope, listing-image angle, and bundle opportunity.
3. Prioritize ideas that can become visually strong digital downloads.
4. Avoid copying current sellers.
5. Allow the user to select an idea and immediately continue into the full workflow without restarting intake.

## Automation-System Product Mode

When the product itself is an automation ZIP for the seller's customers:

- Explain the customer transformation without calling it a skill.
- Describe it as an automation system, digital workflow, creator system, product factory, or toolkit.
- Include installation instructions.
- Include a Start Here file.
- Include safe setup steps.
- Include example commands.
- Include a thank-you page with a clearly marked space for the seller's access or download link when requested.
- Create listing images explaining what the system creates and how the buyer uses it.

## Safety, Originality, and Marketplace Integrity

- Create original content and visuals.
- Do not imitate a living artist's style by name.
- Do not use copyrighted characters, celebrity likenesses, protected logos, or trademarked franchises without permission.
- Do not copy competitor products, descriptions, photographs, mockups, or page layouts.
- Flag trademark or licensing concerns and replace them with an original concept.
- Do not create deceptive income claims or imply Etsy guarantees sales.
- Do not include malware, credential collection, hidden scripts, or unsafe installation behavior.

## Completion Standard

A task is complete only when all requested deliverables are finished, checked, organized, and ready for the seller to use.

Do not claim completion when only a concept, cover, sample, first page, first batch, or preview has been created.

## Mandatory Image Generation for Visual Deliverables

Whenever this skill creates or updates a customer-facing visual, use ChatGPT's built-in image-generation tool for every finished visual. This rule applies to covers, interiors, planner pages, worksheets, printables, illustrations, graphics, listing images, mockups, ads, product scenes, thumbnails, storyboards, video keyframes, and any other visible asset promised by the skill or requested by the user.

- Treat one requested finished page or asset as one separate image-generation call and one separate delivered file. Never combine several requested finished pages into a collage, contact sheet, grid, or single image.
- Do not replace required image generation with programmatic SVG, HTML/CSS, Python or Node drawing, shape-built document layouts, vector-only construction, blank templates, or placeholder previews.
- Use code and deterministic tools only after image generation for tasks such as resizing, cropping, background removal, text correction, PDF assembly, hyperlinking, file packaging, and quality checks. These tools must not create the finished visual instead of image generation.
- Use every user-supplied reference image that applies, preserve approved identity and style details, and inspect generated outputs before delivery.
- Generate the complete requested set in numbered batches. Do not stop after a sample, cover, first page, or first batch.
- If built-in image generation is unavailable or fails, explain the blocker instead of silently switching to a code-built visual.
- For a genuinely text-only, audio-only, technical, review, packaging, or utility task with no requested visual deliverable, do not generate unrelated images. If the task includes even one customer-facing visual, this mandatory rule applies to that visual.

## Mandatory 567 × 726 Page Size

Whenever this skill creates a planner, e-book, workbook, journal, guide, activity book, coloring book, busy book, KDP-style page product, or any other page-based digital product, every finished page must be exactly **567 × 726 pixels in portrait orientation**.

- Use 567 × 726 px for the front cover, back cover, title page, dividers, interior pages, worksheets, bonus pages, and every other page in the product.
- Keep the entire product at one identical canvas size. Never mix page dimensions or aspect ratios within one product.
- Do not substitute US Letter, A4, square, landscape, print-trim, or automatically inferred dimensions for these page-based products.
- If a reference uses a different size or ratio, recompose it onto a full 567 × 726 px canvas without stretching, side bars, exterior mats, or visible background around a smaller page.
- The canvas must equal the functional page from edge to edge. Do not place a smaller worksheet or cover on top of another background.
- Preserve the 567 × 726 px page size during PDF assembly, hyperlinking, export, packaging, and later corrections. Do not add padding or resize individual pages.
- Verify every finished page's pixel dimensions before delivery. Correct only the affected page when a size check fails.
- This rule controls product pages only. Etsy listing images, promotional graphics, mockups, social posts, and videos may use their own requested marketing dimensions.
