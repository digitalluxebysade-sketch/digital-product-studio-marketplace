# Start Here

Use this automation when you want to create a complete digital product for Etsy rather than only receiving ideas or a description.

## Fast command

Create a complete Etsy-ready [PRODUCT TYPE] for [TARGET BUYER]. Use a [THEME] style and [DIMENSIONS]. Include [COUNT] separate pages or assets, a compiled final product, five listing images, three mockups, SEO listing copy, customer instructions, license terms, and a final ZIP. Do not stop after a sample or first batch.

## Example

Create a complete Etsy-ready 24-page Soft Life Budget Planner for beginner budgeters. Use a soft pink, cream, and brown coquette theme at 567 × 726 px. Generate all 24 pages separately, compile a high-quality PDF, create five listing images, three tablet mockups, title, description, tags, customer instructions, personal-use license, and a final ZIP.
