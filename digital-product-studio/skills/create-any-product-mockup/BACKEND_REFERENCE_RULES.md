# Backend Visual Reference Library Rules

## Mandatory pre-generation retrieval

The mockup workflow must search `reference-index.json` before generating. It must select **2-5 active references**, never the full library.

### Retrieval priority

1. Product family and construction
2. Audience: adult women, adult men, children, mannequin/product-only
3. Requested aesthetic
4. Scene and environment
5. Lighting and color palette
6. Pose and framing
7. Commercial purpose: Pinterest pin, Etsy listing, product hero, close-up, or lifestyle image

### Reference-role assignment

Every selected image must be assigned one or more roles:

- Product construction
- Garment silhouette
- Surface-decoration method
- Scene/environment
- Lighting
- Pose/framing
- Styling
- Color palette
- Prop styling
- Device/screen composition

Do not copy unrelated elements from a reference.

## Pinterest-ready output standard

The final result must be visually curated, save-worthy, commercially useful, and product focused. It should feel like a polished Pinterest inspiration image, boutique campaign, or premium Etsy mockup rather than a generic AI catalog image.

Preserve:

- Soft intentional lighting
- Clean composition and negative space
- Product visibility
- Coordinated props and palette
- Aesthetic scene continuity
- Correct product construction and materials
- Natural fabric folds, reflections, shadows, and surface curvature

Avoid:

- Generic stiff poses
- Random props
- Harsh or muddy lighting
- Cluttered backgrounds
- Warped garments
- Broken hands or anatomy
- Floating straps or decoration
- Misspelled text
- Accidental logos or labels
- Marketplace UI, Pinterest UI, browser chrome, watermarks, or Deal badges
- Copying a reference person's exact face or identity
- Reproducing copyrighted characters, trademarks, or graphics from reference photos

## Product modes

1. **Blank mockup** - plain product with a clean printable area and no accidental graphics.
2. **Apply my design** - accurately place the user's artwork with perspective, folds, curvature, reflections, and print boundaries.
3. **Create the product** - create the full product concept using the selected references for construction, mood, and scene.

## Child references

Child apparel outputs must remain age appropriate, neutral, comfortable, and commercially styled. Never use adult-coded posing, makeup, or styling.

## Separate image rule

One requested mockup equals one separate image-generation output. Use batches when needed, but do not combine completed mockups into a collage unless specifically requested.

## Pose and framing support

Treat pose, framing, and camera angle as core visual decisions.

When a request involves a human model, mannequin, handheld product, or product presentation angle:
- retrieve at least one relevant pose/framing reference when available
- preserve the general body language, crop style, orientation, and camera distance
- keep the product visible and commercially useful
- avoid awkward or stiff poses

Preferred pose outcomes include full-body, three-quarter, waist-up, seated, walking, leaning, mirror-selfie, handheld, flatlay, mannequin-front, mannequin-side, product-only hero, and back-view compositions where relevant.
