## Overlap decisions


Listing copy versus Etsy automation: useful specialization versus orchestration. The copy skill writes for an existing product or concept; Etsy automation owns a complete Etsy package. Resume only incomplete stages.

Mockups versus listing images: useful specialization. Mockups emphasize product scenes and accurate presentation; listing images organize a merchandising sequence. A mockup can fill a listing-manifest slot without a second generation.

E-book versus editable ebook/workbook: useful specialization. Use the e-book generator for a designed book and reference layout; use the editable pair for teaching plus application exercises. Recipe content routes to the recipe specialist.

Children’s versus coloring versus puzzles: useful specialization. A pure coloring book routes to coloring with age-appropriate complexity. Alphabet/tracing/early-learning routes to children’s. Puzzle mechanics and answer keys route to puzzles even for children.

Complete low-content books versus interiors: useful specialization. Interiors handle the inside-page scope; a journal/logbook product uses low-content. Do not recreate finished interiors just to invoke both.

Factory versus coordinator: orchestration. The factory already owns product families, the ledger, and custom production. The coordinator only selects owners and resolves handoffs. Seasonal collections retain the seasonal owner.

Branding versus social/YouTube kits: useful specialization. Reuse the brand kit’s approved identity; produce the larger social or channel kit only when requested.

Teacher packs versus worksheet variants: useful specialization. Teacher owns the classroom package; variants own meaningful differentiation when needed.

Interactive invitations versus card suites: use the birthday-specific Canva workflow when the envelope-opening birthday reveal is central; use the general Canva workflow for other interactive or scrolling event invitations. Use the wedding stationery workflow for a full wedding paper-goods system, and use the invitation card suite for flat printable or digital card sets.

No true duplicate skills were consolidated. Shared production rules remain in their original files.
